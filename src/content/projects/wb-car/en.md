---
title: "Vehicle Branding for WB"
---

Made in 2 days in 2024

The task was to create a wrap concept for regional delivery vehicles and attract new entrepreneurs to the marketplace.

WB had no branding for the vehicles that deliver parcels to pickup points, while other services have recognisable cars. We needed to choose a colour palette and an overall positioning style that sets WB apart from its competitors. We also had to account for technical constraints that allow only 40% of the vehicle surface to be used. With this in mind, a bubble style was chosen for the wrap, and its principles can be carried over to branding on other media.

Sometimes the space around the bubbles can also be wrapped. In that case, the recognisable WB gradient is used as the background to stay consistent with the brand style. For the bubbles, a new blueberry gradient was chosen to feel fresh. Each bubble is logically linked to its neighbours, together forming an information system on the vehicle. An additional option introduces generative patterns that complement the bubbles.
