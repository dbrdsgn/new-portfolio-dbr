Made in 30 days in 2023

Rebranding and packaging for ERA

ERA lighting products are in high demand.

The task was to rebrand the company and set the rules for updating the entire product line.

Now the bulbs are easy to tell apart by colour temperature, wattage and other key specs — they are always in plain sight.

The branding covers both the outside of the box and its insides. The bulb carries the new mark, and the inside of the lid lists the short specs.

Stickers complete the updated visual style.

Blocks of boxes on the shelf now look more distinctive and playful.
